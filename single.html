ABOUT / ALLEN MIRZOYAN

An engineer in
the making.

I'm Allen, a Computer Science graduate from Maastricht University. I like building software, but I also want to understand why it behaves the way it does.

What I'm doing now

I'm spending time with retrieval-augmented generation, local language models, chess analysis, and robotics. The topics are different, but the motivation is the same: turning abstractions into systems I can inspect, test, and improve.

How I learn

By building. AtlasLLM started as a small experiment with embeddings and grew into a document-question-answering pipeline. A* search became easier to understand once I could see maps and ROS topics rather than only read pseudocode. Chess analytics raised harder questions about how to measure something as slippery as a player's style.

What I'm looking for

Early-career software engineering and applied AI work where I can contribute, learn from strong engineers, and get better at building reliable systems.
